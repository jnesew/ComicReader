# ComicViewer 1.1 device test checklist

Use at least one three-issue series, one standalone comic, one unavailable middle issue, a short
CBZ, a tall webtoon-style archive, and a PDF. Test both LTR and RTL reading direction where noted.

## Paged series navigation

- In Single page, advance past the final page of issue 1 and confirm issue 2 opens on page 1.
- From page 1 of issue 2, go back and confirm issue 1 opens on its final page.
- Repeat both directions in Two-page spread. Confirm the backward transition opens the spread that
  contains the final page, including odd page counts and standalone landscape pages.
- Repeat with RTL direction and confirm page placement changes without reversing issue order.
- Confirm the first issue reports **Start of series**, the final issue reports **End of series**,
  and navigation never wraps.
- Make the adjacent issue unavailable. Confirm the current issue remains open and the unavailable
  message identifies the attempted direction.
- Confirm Android system Back returns to the library from every reader position.

## Continuous series scrolling

- Use a series of at least six single-page issues shorter than the viewport (such as Little
  Sammy Sneeze). Open the first, middle, and final issue separately; scroll through the series
  in both directions. Confirm the buffer never stalls at two issues or changes the opened issue
  during loading. Repeat zoomed out and in portrait/landscape, including Previous navigation.
- Open the middle issue in Continuous mode and scroll normally through the next-issue separator.
  Confirm no tap or button is required and controls remain in Continuous mode.
- Scroll back through the previous-issue separator and confirm the viewport does not jump when the
  earlier issue is inserted.
- Fling rapidly in both directions. Confirm loading appears inline when necessary, pages remain in
  order, and the viewport does not jump when the three-issue buffer shifts.
- At each boundary, confirm the reader title, page label, slider range, and bookmark state refer to
  the issue currently at the top of the viewport.
- Add different bookmarks in adjacent issues, cross the boundary, and verify both bookmark lists.
- Close and reopen after crossing a boundary. Confirm the active issue and its reading position
  restore correctly.
- Inspect the library before crossing into a prefetched issue. Confirm prefetch alone does not mark
  it started or change its progress.
- Make the next or previous issue unavailable and confirm it is shown inline and never skipped.
- Revoke access or remove an adjacent file before it loads. Confirm an inline retry state appears,
  tapping it retries, and the current issue remains readable.
- Switch to Single page or Two-page spread while adjacent issues are buffered. Confirm the active
  issue and page remain selected and memory use settles after the buffered readers close.
- Leave the reader or open a different comic during boundary loading. Confirm the obsolete load
  does not replace the newly opened comic.

## Edit comic

- Open **Edit comic** from a title menu and from the reader. Confirm both show Comic title,
  Grouping, Series, and Issue number with persistent labels.
- Change the comic title and save. Confirm it updates in Titles view, Series view, search results,
  the reader title, and issue separators.
- Reopen the comic, rescan its folder, and restart the app. Confirm the custom title remains.
- If an exact duplicate is reconciled with a manual import, confirm the custom title remains.
- Tap **Use original title**, save, and confirm subsequent reopen/rescan updates the detected title.
- Choose an existing series suggestion and set an issue number. Confirm ordering and continuous
  navigation use the new assignment.
- Create a new manual series, choose Automatic grouping, and choose Standalone in separate passes.
  Confirm each choice takes effect independently of the comic title.
- Cancel after changing every field and confirm no metadata changes are saved.
- Confirm editing metadata never renames, moves, or changes the source comic file.

## Reader menu and regression checks

- Confirm the reader menu order is Jump to page, Bookmarks, favorite, Edit comic, Reading
  direction, Reader options.
- Confirm **Add or open comics**, **Background color**, **Hardware shortcuts**, and **Hide controls**
  are absent from the reader menu.
- Open Reader options from the reader and library. Confirm Background color and Hardware shortcuts
  are available and work from both entry points.
- Confirm a center tap still hides and restores controls, including when auto-hide is disabled.
- Smoke-test page preview, zoom, rotation, app pause/resume, folder rescan, unavailable-item forget,
  and bulk confirmed-missing cleanup after the database upgrade.
