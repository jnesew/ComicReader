Read your own comics offline with ComicViewer, a small Android reader for CBZ/ZIP, PDF, image-based EPUB and loose image-folder comics.

Choose single-page, two-page spread or continuous scrolling. Continue across issues in a series, remember your reading position, jump to a page and use bookmarks. Check the current issue’s creator credits and publication details without leaving the reader. In continuous mode, volume keys scroll by one viewport with overlap. Adjust zoom, lock zoom gestures and choose left-to-right, right-to-left or automatic reading direction.

Organize local files in a cover grid with search, sorting, comic and series favorites, and series groups. Show covers alone or reserve one or two title lines, with reading status aligned across cards. Open a series overview with Continue and expandable details for that same issue. Import files, scan library folders, or read a folder of loose images as one comic. ComicInfo can provide descriptions, credits, publication dates, covers and reading-direction hints. The folder manager distinguishes library folders from image comics and can refresh image pages while retaining positions and bookmarks by image identity. Routine scans stay silent; incomplete scans and lost access show a notice. Edit titles and series information without changing the source files. Choose Light, Dark or System default appearance for the library.

ComicViewer works without an account, network access, advertising, analytics or Google Play services. Files stay on your device. It requests no Android permissions and uses access granted through the system file picker.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. EPUB support is for image-based comics; this is not a reflowable text ebook reader. The app is available in English and Finnish. No comics are bundled.

Screenshot artwork: public-domain newspaper comics by Winsor McCay, Gustave Verbeek and Richard F. Outcault. Exact source records and capture details: https://github.com/jnesew/ComicReader/blob/main/docs/STORE-ASSETS.md.
